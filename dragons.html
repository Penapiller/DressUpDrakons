Prepping layer images (click to expand)
Prepping layers: each layer is a PNG with a transparent background. It's loaded at its exact original pixel dimensions — nothing is resized, resampled, or compressed. Paint every colorable region on a layer as one flat solid color (no anti-aliasing) — the tool groups matching colors into sections you can repaint. A layer can be a base coat, a line-art layer (its "sections" will just be your outline colors — recolor those too if you like), a pattern, or an item like a bow or saddle. Order them in the list with the ↑ / ↓ arrows; the top of the list draws in front. The canvas auto-sizes to the largest layer you've added, and every layer is anchored to its top-left corner — so for pieces to line up (a hat sitting on a head, for example), export all your layers from the same canvas size in your art program even if individual pieces only use part of it.
